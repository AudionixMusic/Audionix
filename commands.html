Music Commands

/play <url>
Plays music from a YouTube or SoundCloud URL.
/search <query>
Searches YouTube and SoundCloud for the given keywords and shows the top results.
/select <number>
Selects a song from the previous search results by number.
/showqueue
Displays the current song queue.
/pause
Pauses the currently playing audio.
/unpause
Resumes the paused audio.
/stop
Stops playback and clears the queue.
/mute
Mutes the bot's audio in the voice channel.
/unmute
Unmutes the bot's audio in the voice channel.
/volume <1-100>
Sets the playback volume between 1% and 100%.
/loop
Toggles loop mode for the currently playing track.
